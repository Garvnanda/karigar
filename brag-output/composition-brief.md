# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Karigar

## Objective
A 3½-minute cinematic, narrated explainer for Karigar, made for a hackathon pitch. It is motion graphics only and has no pricing.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/` (generated by `brag-output/build.py`)
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape, 1920x1080, 30 fps
- Duration: 214 s. The user overrode the 15–25 s default with `--duration 210` and asked for a 3–4 min explainer.

## Source Material
- Project root: the karigar repo. Read: README.md, docs/STATUS.md, docs/nonilion.md, data/rate_card.json, karigar/packs/jewellery/{brief,pricing}.py
- Product name: Karigar
- Strongest claim: "Bring your old gold, and leave with a new design that carries exactly that gold, ready to cast."
- Copy that must appear verbatim:
  - Customer request: "Nani ka purana 18.4g 22K kangan hai, naya beaded kada banana hai" (README)
  - Follow-up: "Aap anguthi, choodi ya pendant mein se kya banwana chahenge?" (real `brief.parse()` output)
  - Refusal: drawn from the real `pricing.plan()` output for "purani 22K chain 3g se naya kangan banao": "below the minimum for a bangle that survives daily wear" and the options pendant / 14K / add a little gold
  - Agent names and zones: Sakhi (Counter), Jauhari, Naqqash (Design studio), Karigar (Workshop), Chitrakar (Photo studio)

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: cinematic
- Direction: a trailer-scale jeweller's workshop. Velvet black, gold light, big serif caps.
- User constraints added mid-run:
  - No pricing at all. Jauhari's zone is shown as "Gold desk", not "Pricing desk".
  - Minimal technical detail.
  - Focus on the visuals and on why the idea is good, different and useful.
- Avoid: rupee figures, the equation and solver detail, generic SaaS language.

## Visual Identity
- Ground #120D0A; ink #F3E9D6; gold accent #E0B25A; deep gold #9C7230; ruby #C24B58 (refusal); emerald #3FA37C (follow-up)
- Fonts (all bundled): Playfair Display 900 caps for display, EB Garamond for narrative, IBM Plex Mono for chat and labels
- Drawn assets (SVG): kangan, chain, beaded kada, solitaire ring, lotus pendant, balance scale, agent icons

## Storyboard
See `brag-plan.md`. Scene starts in seconds: 0 Drawer · 17 Guess · 34 Reveal · 48 Request · 62 Five agents · 91 Magic · 111 Outputs · 128 Says no · 147 Asks · 159 Why it matters · 177 What's next · 203 Outro.

## Audio
- Voice: Kokoro af_heart, generated locally. Each scene has its own clip, and the timed scenes were split by sentence so reveals land on the spoken word (`vo-timing.json`).
- Music: vol-12 (0–117 s), crossfaded into vol-9 (from 114.5 s). The bed sits at 0.30 and ducks to 0.12 under narration through `data-automation` volume lanes.
- SFX (66 cues): soft impacts on reveals, drops on agent and roadmap reveals, card sounds on design cards, keypresses on typed requests, a wood hit on the refusal, bells on the title and outro.
- Audio-reactive: the background gold glow's opacity follows music RMS. It is sampled at 4 Hz and baked in at build time.
- Beat sync: not used. The film is narration-led, so timing follows the voice instead of the music cues.

## Rebuild
`cd brag-output && python build.py && cd composition && npx hyperframes check && npx hyperframes render --quality delivery --output ../brag.mp4`
